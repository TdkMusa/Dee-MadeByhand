OUR STORY

Simple. Natural. Handmade.

Dee-Made byhand is a small South African self-care brand. Everything we sell is blended, filled and packed by hand in small batches: herbal infusions, bath teas, bath salts, bath oils and gift sets.

We keep our range simple on purpose. Flowers and herbs like hibiscus, chamomile, lavender, rose and calendula do the work, and we would rather make a few things carefully than many things quickly.

Every order is packed by hand, which means the person who made your tea is the same person who wrapped it.
